Conduct

PD Rules 1-16

Know the rules. Follow the rules. Represent Chico PD.

How punishment works

The rules are grouped by how serious they are:

  • Rules 1-3, dismissal: false allegations, detaining without cause and attacking civilians without a roleplay reason.
  • Rules 4-10, 1 reprimand: disrespect, force without warning, ignoring superiors, misusing vehicles and insults.
  • Rules 11-15, 1 warning: arresting without a reason or screenshot, ignoring Discord, not sharing arrest info and poor roleplay.

Rule 16: 2 warnings make 1 reprimand, and 3 reprimands mean removal from the position of police officer.
