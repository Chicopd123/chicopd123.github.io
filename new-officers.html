New Officers

Everyone starts unbadged. Here's what that means and how you earn your badge.

Getting your badge

  1. Add your macros and get them verified by a vested officer or above.
  2. Do 50,000 XP in patrol.
  3. Complete RP1 and RP2 with a vested officer. Watch the RP videos.
  4. Get logged. If you passed your training, you'll get your badge in less than 24 hours.

Until then you can't go out alone, take or spawn a PD car, be on Floor 1, make arrests, or leave your patrol vehicle to talk to the public.

Inactive for 7 days? You are removed from the PD automatically.
