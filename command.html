Chain of Command

Every rank in Chico PD, from the Chief Federal General down to recruits, and what each one does.

How the system works

Everyone starts at rank 1 and works their way up to rank 13.

  1. Get your badge

    Everyone starts as a Recruit (rank 1). Add your macros, reach 50,000 XP and pass RP1 and RP2 to get your badge and become a Sergeant.

  2. Get off Floor 1

    New Sergeants work Floor 1 at the metal detectors. Send 5 bodycams to a PAB member to be cleared for patrols.

  3. Get your vest

    On patrols, show 5 bodycams and reach the next rank's XP, then apply for the vest test. Colonels and above can test you.

  4. Keep moving up

    The Chief and Deputy Federal Generals decide promotions. The PD rules apply to every rank, from Recruit to Chief.
