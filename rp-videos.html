Before you start

To get your badge you have to pass RP1 and RP2 in the game with a vested officer. Watch the videos below first so you know exactly what to do.

Unlocks when you have

  • 50,000 XP in patrol
  • Passed your macros, checked by a vested officer or above

Then find a vested officer and ask if they're available to do your RPs. Pass them all and you'll get your badge in less than 24 hours.
